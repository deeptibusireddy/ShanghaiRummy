# Shanghai Rummy Nights Support

Shanghai Rummy Nights is an iPad card game for two to six players. You can
play a solo table with bots or invite people to a live table through Apple
Game Center.

## Common questions

### Game Center is not connected

Confirm that the iPad is signed in to Game Center in **Settings > Game
Center**, then reopen Shanghai Rummy Nights and choose **Sign In to Game
Center** from the home screen.

### An invited player cannot join

Each human player needs a separate Apple Account signed in to Game Center and
a compatible version of the app. Return to **Create a Table**, confirm the
human seats, and reopen the Game Center guest chooser.

### Resume a saved solo table

Bot-only games can be saved from the bookmark button during normal play.
Choose **Resume Game** from the saved-table card on the home screen. Live Game
Center tables cannot be saved or resumed.

### Change or disable turn sounds

Choose **Sounds** on the home screen or use the speaker control during a game.

### Remove local game data

Discard a saved game from its home-screen card. Deleting the app removes all
remaining local saves and preferences.

## Contact support

Open a
[support issue](https://github.com/deeptibusireddy/ShanghaiRummy/issues/new)
with the app version, iPad model, iPadOS version, and a brief description of
what happened. Do not include passwords, Apple Account details, or other
sensitive information.
