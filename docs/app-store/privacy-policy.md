# Shanghai Rummy Nights Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 18, 2026

Shanghai Rummy Nights is designed to play without a developer-operated
account, server, advertising network, or analytics service.

## Information handled by the app

- **Solo games and settings:** Saved solo games, sound preferences, and other
  app settings are stored locally on the iPad. They are not sent to the
  developer.
- **Game Center multiplayer:** When a player chooses a live table, the app
  uses Apple Game Center to authenticate players, arrange the match, display
  Game Center names, and exchange gameplay messages. The developer does not
  operate a server that receives or retains those messages or player
  identifiers.
- **No advertising or tracking:** The app does not include advertising,
  cross-app tracking, third-party analytics, or data brokers.
- **No purchases or payment data:** The app does not offer in-app purchases
  and does not receive payment information.

Apple processes information used by Game Center according to
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Retention and deletion

Local settings remain on the iPad until they are changed, reset, or the app is
deleted. A saved solo game can also be discarded from the home screen.
Deleting the app removes its locally stored data. Game Center information is
managed through the player's Apple Account and Game Center settings.

Because the developer does not maintain user accounts or a database of
personal information, there is no developer-held account or personal-data
record to delete.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change.
The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
